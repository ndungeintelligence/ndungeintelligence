What does "infrastructure intelligence" actually mean?+
We start from your business problem, match it to a proven emerging-technology pattern — AI agents, data platforms, new payment rails — and then build the specific infrastructure that makes the pattern run in your business. Not advice in a deck; working systems.
Do we need strong data foundations to start?+
No. Part of the Assess phase is finding out what you have and what's missing. Many of our builds begin with getting product, customer or transaction data into a usable shape.
How quickly will we see results?+
You get a costed blueprint and a working proof on the critical path within two weeks. Production builds typically run four to eight weeks, with something demonstrable every week.
Can you work with payment methods that aren't fully online?+
Yes — that's a speciality. Our Hybrid Payments Bridge reconciles cash, EFT and in-person payments alongside online checkout. We use Paystack today and design for local rails such as instant EFT and PayShap.
How is pricing structured?+
Fixed prices per phase. Small, well-defined engagements can be booked and paid online on our Engage page; larger builds get a written proposal with a fixed quote after the Sprint.
Do you work outside South Africa?+
Yes. We're remote-native and work with clients across Africa and internationally. Our infrastructure is designed for African markets first, but it isn't limited to them.

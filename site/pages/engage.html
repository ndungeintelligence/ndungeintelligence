Payments

How paying works.

Is online payment secure?+
Payments are processed by Paystack, a licensed payment provider. Your card details go directly to Paystack — we never see or store them.
Will I get a receipt?+
Yes. A receipt is emailed as soon as the payment is confirmed. Reply with your company details if you need a formal tax invoice.
What if the engagement isn't a fit?+
We confirm scope with you before any work starts. If it isn't the right fit, we'll say so straight away and agree a fair outcome with you. The Advisory Session fee is credited against any project booked within 30 days.
Can I pay in instalments for larger builds?+
Larger builds are invoiced in milestones agreed in the proposal. Start with a Sprint deposit or simply send us a brief.
